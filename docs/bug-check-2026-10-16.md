## 2. Security (confirmed)

| # | Where | What goes wrong |
|---|---|---|
| 11 ✅ | Admin login (`Qspot-API/routes/admin.js` ~17, `server.js` ~85) | **No limit on password guesses for the admin login.** Student sign-in is limited; admin sign-in isn't. One correct guess gives full control for 24 hours. |
| 12 ✅ | Unreleased videos (`Qspot-API/routes/videos.js` ~66) | The video link is hidden for upcoming lessons, but the **storage key is still sent**. Someone who looks can build the file address and watch the video early. |

**Weaknesses in how sign-in works.** These connect to the open item "OTP verify step not built".
- Anyone can register **someone else's phone number** with their own date of birth. The real owner is then locked out.
- Anyone who knows a student's number can send 5 wrong tries every 15 minutes. That **keeps the student locked out**, for example just before an exam.
- A date of birth can be guessed in about 1–2 days at the current limit.
- Students can **change their own class** in their profile. That opens quizzes, assignments and certificates meant for other classes.
- The quiz timer relies on the time the app reports. This is needed for offline quizzes, but a modified app could claim more time or a faster finish.

## 4. Reported by the review, not yet double-checked by me

The reviewers traced these and they look likely, but I haven't read each one myself yet.
- Leaving a lesson before pressing play resets its resume point to 0.
- "Start practice" from the lesson Details sheet usually does nothing.
- The "per question" and "overall + per question" practice timer modes don't work as the admin options describe.
- Opening an upcoming lesson from a teacher's page or from the Home hero card opens a different video.
- The "Video completed, answer the questions" popup appears again on every re-watch.
- An uploaded (non-YouTube) video that fails to load shows a spinner forever, with no error or retry.
- Watch time is lost when a progress update fails, or at 1.5×/2× speed. Some students may never reach "completed" and stay locked out of practice.
- A quiz the server refused can, in one rare path, get sent again with blank answers. The student then gets 0 and is locked out.
- Assignment uploads time out after 15 seconds, which is too short for voice recordings on slow networks.
- The notification inbox only shows the latest 50, and the unread badge only counts those.
- The reminder bell on a class that already happened today silently does nothing.
- Live class reminders say "New video available" and open nothing when tapped.
- Logging out doesn't clear saved chapter and lesson lists. The next student briefly sees them, or keeps seeing them when offline.
- Chapter covers disappear when the app shows saved (offline) chapters.
- Files uploaded with a form that is then rejected stay in storage forever.
